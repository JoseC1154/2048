Action history

    Tap to select · double tap to flip · hold a block to select its touching group · drag for grid movement. Arrow keys move the focused block, or the first selected block; joined blocks move together. Each cell traveled costs MP. When energy runs out, a charged note spawns and grants 3 MP; use it in a chord for extra energy. Beginner has one board-lock rescue; Advanced has none. The semitone register changes after every successful move or flip and determines the next flip (−12 to +12). JOIN requires edge contact. Play any connected major or minor triad to score. Each played chord is replaced with three new random notes in the cleared cells. The game continues until the survival rules end it.
